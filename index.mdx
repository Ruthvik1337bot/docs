## Get Up and Running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Argus in your browser">
    Navigate to the Argus page in any supported browser (Chrome or Edge recommended). No sign-up or installation is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow microphone access">
    When prompted, click **Allow** so Argus can hear your voice. You can also type commands if you prefer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say 'Hey Argus'">
    Speak the wake word to activate the agent. The orb will glow and Argus will start listening for your command.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a command">
    Try saying **"open YouTube"** or **"search Google for weather today"**. Argus responds with voice and instantly acts.
  </Step>
</Steps>
